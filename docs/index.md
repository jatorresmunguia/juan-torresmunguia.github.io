# Juan Torres Munguía, Ph.D.


I am a Statistician specialized in the management, analysis, and
visualization of data related to humanitarian emergencies and most
vulnerable populations.

My work integrates statistical methods, machine learning algorithms, and
data science techniques to better understand how individuals, families,
and communities experience, adapt to, and cope with phenomena such as
armed conflict, violence, climate change, poverty, and displacement.

As an R-enthusiast, I love coding to optimize and streamline my work.
Check out my [Blog](../blog/index.qmd), where I share insights on using
R for the study of humanitarian and development data.
